# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different levels of technical and accounting experience. Critique proposals and code rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information and persistent disruption are not acceptable. Do not post financial records, personal identifiers or credentials while asking for help.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or remove access. They should act proportionately and explain decisions when privacy permits.

For a concern, contact a maintainer through an available private channel. If no private channel is available, request one in a public issue without naming affected individuals or disclosing the incident details. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This original policy applies to project-managed issues, pull requests and discussions. It does not promise moderation of unrelated third-party services.
