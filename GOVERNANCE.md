# Governance

The initial maintainer is [alex-engvall](https://github.com/alex-engvall). The public Project remains independent of Kollino and Fortnox and is intended to be a useful, freely available community tool. **Easy Today AB** is the legal recipient of licenses granted under the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). This does not make Fortnox, Kollino or Easy Today AB the publisher of an official Fortnox product.

## Decisions

- Approved product choices live in [the decision register](docs/decisions.md).
- Implementation details may be refined through scoped PRs when they preserve those choices.
- Changes to licensing, supported trust boundaries, approval defaults, data retention principles or stable-release coverage require an explicit maintainer decision and updated documentation.
- Record the reason, alternatives, migration impact and date for a decision change. Do not leave contradictory current instructions across files.

All contributions are reviewed. Maintainers can invite additional maintainers after sustained, constructive contributions; no automatic access is granted by contribution count.

## Community contributions and commercial licensing

External copyrightable contributions require an accepted [CLA](CLA.md) once the signing workflow is activated and verified. Contributors **retain copyright**. A signed CLA grants Easy Today AB explicit non-exclusive rights to license covered contributions separately, including commercially and under proprietary terms. Accepted Community contributions remain available in this public repository under **AGPL-3.0-only**. The CLA does not relicense any previous contribution retroactively or remove third-party obligations.

Optional hosted, support or premium offerings may be developed separately by Easy Today AB, but none is announced or implied to exist by this policy. This project does not require a customer to buy one in order to use the AGPL-licensed software. The community project remains open to contributions and independently installable. The GitHub CLA signing service and required check must be configured according to the [maintainer runbook](docs/maintainers/cla-setup.md) before this policy is enforced.

## Releases and responsibility

Development is public. Prereleases describe their supported capabilities and known gaps. A stable release requires the gates in [release policy](docs/releases.md). No service-level agreement or central hosting is implied.

Commercial use and paid distribution/hosting are allowed under AGPL. The project does not operate a resale-permission scheme. Original Community releases are intended to remain free of charge. Separate commercial offerings must be clearly distinguished from the publicly AGPL-licensed Community software, and must not obscure what users receive or their AGPL rights.

## Project conduct

Disagreements should be resolved in issues or PRs using evidence and user impact. Maintainers apply the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), explaining moderation decisions where doing so does not expose private information.
